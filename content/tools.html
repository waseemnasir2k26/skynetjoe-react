Reason we ship these

Why we put real tools behind a no-email-gate page

Three reasons, in order. First — trust. The fastest way to prove an agency knows the work is to show the work, running, free. Second — feedback. Public tools get poked at in ways internal demos never do. We've shipped four real client improvements from edge cases caught on the free AEO Score Check. Third — we dogfood our own AEO stack. Every tool is its own indexable page, its own JSON-LD entry, and its own citation hook. If our stack works on us, it works on the client.
