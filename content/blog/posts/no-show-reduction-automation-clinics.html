An empty appointment slot is a cost you cannot get back. The room, the equipment and the staff are all still there, but no one is in the chair.

This article explains the no-show killer workflow from our short video. It sends confirmations at the right moment, follows up harder with chronic no-shows, and the instant someone cancels, the slot is offered to your wait list.

About the numbers. The video says the average clinic loses about 18% of appointments to no-shows, and uses roughly 200 dollars per empty chair as an example. Both are often-quoted or illustrative figures, not our client data. Use your own appointment value when you do the maths.

What does a no-show really cost?

Take an example clinic with 100 appointments a week, an 18 percent no-show rate and 200 dollars per slot. That is 18 empty slots, or 3,600 dollars a week of capacity that did not earn. These numbers are an example, not a promise, but they show why even a modest improvement is worth a look.

Phone tag is the hidden cost. Reception spends time calling to confirm, leaving messages and trying to refill gaps, and that time is rarely recorded.

There is a fairness angle too. Patients on a waiting list are often the ones who most need an earlier slot, so refilling cancellations quickly helps them as well as the practice.

How the no-show killer works

Confirm at the right moments, chase the likely no-shows harder, and refill cancelled slots from a wait list automatically.

Timing matters more than volume. Too many reminders annoy people, too few miss the window. We set the schedule around your typical lead time.

For health-related businesses, keep messages free of clinical detail. A time, a place and a reply option are enough.

What you need to get started

  • A booking system with an API or calendar feed.
  • Patient or client phone numbers with permission to message them.
  • A wait list, even a simple one.
  • Your cancellation policy so the wording matches it.

Honest limits

  • It reduces no-shows. It will not eliminate them.
  • Messaging health clients carries privacy duties, so keep content minimal and follow your local rules.
  • If your booking system has no API, we may need a workaround.
  • A wait list only helps if people are on it and respond quickly.
