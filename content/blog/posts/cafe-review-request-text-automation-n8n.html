Why do cafes struggle to collect reviews?

A great flat white earns a smile, not a five-star rating. Owners rarely have time to ask at the till, and a printed QR code on the counter only catches the most motivated customers. Meanwhile, a rating of 4.2 against a neighbour's 4.6 can quietly send walk-in tourists elsewhere.

Reviews matter to local search, and they are one of the first things a stranger checks before choosing a cafe. A steady trickle of honest reviews does more than a sudden burst, so the aim is a simple system that asks everyone, every day.

Most people read a text on a phone while doing something else, so the easier the next tap, the more likely they are to follow through. Check your results monthly: how many messages went out, how many reviews arrived, and how many private notes you received. The private notes are often the most useful part, because they tell you what to fix before it shows up as a public one-star review.

Honest limits

  • Review sites have rules against selectively asking only happy customers for public reviews, sometimes called review gating. Offer the public link to everyone and add private feedback alongside it.
  • Walk-in customers who never gave contact details cannot be reached.
  • Never offer rewards in exchange for positive reviews. Many platforms prohibit it.
  • A workflow cannot raise your rating on its own. It only makes asking consistent.

Frequently asked questions

Is it allowed to send customers a review link by text?

Generally yes, if you have permission to message them and you do not pressure them or offer rewards for positive reviews. Check the current rules for each review platform you use.

Should I only send the review link to happy customers?

No. Selectively soliciting only positive reviews is discouraged by major platforms. Send the same invitation to everyone and offer a private feedback option as well.

How do I get customer phone numbers in a cafe?

Common routes are loyalty sign-ups, online or app orders, and reservations. Always get permission when collecting them.

How quickly will my rating improve?

There is no guaranteed timeline. More consistent asking tends to produce more reviews, but the rating still depends on the experience customers had.
