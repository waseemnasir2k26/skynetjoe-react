What is an AI chatbot build?

An AI chatbot is a conversational interface — web, WhatsApp, or voice — built on a large language model (Claude, GPT, or Gemini) with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over your knowledge base plus tool calls for pricing, booking, and CRM lookups. SkynetLabs ships chatbots grounded in your real docs, with structured pricing handoff so the bot cannot hallucinate prices, on Meta Cloud API or web SDK. Containment goal: 60%+ resolution without human handoff.

Voice agent (optional)

Inbound phone via Twilio + Deepgram + GPT-4o + ElevenLabs voice. Books appointments, answers FAQ, transfers complex calls to a real human. Sub-1s latency.

Anti-hallucination guardrails

Bot never quotes a price unless it pulls from your live price file. Never quotes a policy unless it cites the source doc. Falls back to "let me get a human" when retrieval confidence drops below a threshold.

Quality review dashboard

Every conversation scored on resolution, escalation reason, sentiment. Weekly digest: top 10 questions the bot got wrong, top 10 it nailed. Feeds back into the knowledge base.

Questions

Common questions

How is this different from Intercom Fin or Zendesk AI?
Off-the-shelf bots like Fin or Zendesk AI are decent for simple FAQ on a clean help center. They struggle on niche policy questions, on multi-step booking, on quoting from a live price file. We build custom retrieval and tool-calling so the bot can actually act — book a slot, look up an order, issue a refund inside Stripe — rather than just answering text.
What if the bot makes a mistake?
Every conversation is logged. The quality dashboard surfaces low-confidence answers within hours. We tune the system prompt or the retrieval threshold to fix it. For high-stakes domains (healthcare, legal), we set the confidence threshold tight enough that the bot defaults to "let me get a human" rather than guess.
Do we own the bot or rent it?
You own it. The code lives in your GitHub. The knowledge base lives in your Supabase or Pinecone. API keys are yours. You can fire us and run the bot exactly as-is. No platform lock-in.
Will the bot replace our support team?
No — and we'd argue against framing it that way. The bot handles the boring 60-70 percent (where are my shipping details, what's your refund window, do you do gift wrapping). Your support team gets the harder 30 percent with the bot's conversation summary already on their screen. Volume capacity goes up; team size stays the same.
What about languages other than English?
Claude and GPT-4o handle ~25 languages natively at near-English quality. We've shipped bots in English, French, Spanish, Indonesian, Urdu, and Arabic. Knowledge base needs to exist in the target language for retrieval — we'll auto-translate it if you don't have native docs yet.
