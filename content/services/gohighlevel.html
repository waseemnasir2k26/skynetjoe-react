What is a GoHighLevel CRM setup?

GoHighLevel (GHL) is a bundled CRM, marketing automation, SMS/email, calendar, and white-label SaaS platform popular with agencies and service businesses. A SkynetLabs GHL setup includes sub-account provisioning, sales pipelines, drip cadences, calendar workflows, SMS/email templates, and trigger automations rebuilt from scratch (no broken snapshot leftovers). Seven shipped GHL rebuilds for one US insurance retainer client to date.

We bought a snapshot from a YouTuber. Should we keep it?
Usually no. Snapshots are templated for someone else's funnel, and the workflows inside often clash with how you actually sell. We strip what's useful (rarely more than 20 percent), document why we killed the rest, and rebuild the parts that earn their keep.
Do you become our GHL agency reseller?
Only if you want us to. Most clients keep their own agency-tier login because the margin is real. We work inside your account as a sub-user with role-based permissions. After handoff, you can revoke our access in two clicks.
How does this compare to HubSpot or Salesforce?
GHL wins when you sell services, need built-in SMS, and don't want to glue six SaaS tools together. HubSpot wins above 15 seats with complex deal-stage logic. Salesforce wins when compliance and custom objects matter. We're honest if your stage is wrong for GHL and we'll send you to the right tool.
Can you train our staff or do we stay reliant on you?
Training is built into every engagement. You get Loom walkthroughs for every workflow, a one-page SOP per role, and a live training session before handoff. Most clients run their own GHL within 60 days. We stay on retainer only when it earns its fee.
