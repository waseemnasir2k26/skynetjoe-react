What we build

A daily-post engine that earns the swipe

Hook bank rotation

Seven-day hook rotation by weekday: Monday contrarian, Tuesday prompt-exposure, Wednesday list, Thursday client-proof, Friday tool-stack, Saturday personal, Sunday recap. Two A/B variants per day.

Frame-1 stoppable thumbnail

Title card 0.2-3.5 seconds in every video. Bold question, branded colour, your face if you're on camera. So when the algorithm previews it, the viewer pauses instead of swiping.

Kinetic captions

Word-by-word reveal with brand-colour highlight on the emotional word. ASS subtitle format means you can re-skin the whole batch by changing one config.

Trend-aware BGM, not banned audio

We never use TikTok-trending audio that gets stripped on cross-post. We use licensed Mixkit and Epidemic Sound tracks that match the energy of the trending sound without the copyright risk.

Comment-bait closer

Last 2 seconds plant a question or contradiction designed to provoke comments. Comments drive the second wave of distribution. We script the closer per topic.

First-second retention heatmap

Weekly review: which videos lost 50% in second one, which made it to 75% completion. We use the data to retire dead hook formats.

Questions

Common questions

What about shadowbans?
Real shadowbans on TikTok are rare and usually triggered by content policy violations, not posting frequency. If your reach drops, it's almost always the hook format that's gone stale. Our weekly retention review catches that early so we kill dying formats before they tank the whole account.
Do you grow accounts to specific follower counts?
We don't promise follower counts. We do promise a measurable lift in first-frame retention and in median view count, because both are inputs we directly control. Follower count is downstream of those ÃƒÆ’Ã‚Â¢ÃƒÂ¢Ã¢â‚¬Å¡Ã‚Â¬ÃƒÂ¢Ã¢â€šÂ¬Ã‚Â if retention is fixed, followers come.
Can you take over content from my agency?
Yes. We start with a 30-video audit of what your prior agency shipped, identify what worked, what didn't, and the format gap. Most agency-produced TikTok content fails because it's optimised for "polish" rather than "stop-the-scroll".
What's the right posting frequency?
For a channel under 10k followers, one video per day, posted between 6 and 10pm in your target audience's timezone. Past 10k, we lower frequency and raise polish. Daily is rarely sustainable for face-to-camera founders beyond month three without a pipeline.
How do you handle cross-posting to Reels and Shorts?
Same video gets re-uploaded to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts with platform-specific captions and hashtags. We avoid the TikTok watermark by re-rendering for Reels and Shorts. Hashtag set is generated per platform, not copy-pasted.
