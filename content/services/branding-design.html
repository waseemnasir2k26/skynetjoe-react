Questions

Common questions

Do we need a full rebrand or a refresh?
Refresh is right when the existing brand has equity worth keeping (a recognisable logo, a known colour) and the problem is operational drift — inconsistent application, weak templates, no Figma library. Full rebrand is right when the positioning has shifted, the audience has changed, or the visual feels actively wrong. We tell you honestly which one your situation needs.
Will you change our logo?
Only if it earns its keep. About 30% of engagements end with the same logo, refined typography, new palette, and a Figma system that makes the old logo look better in context. Rebranding the logo when the rest of the system is broken is putting fresh paint on a leaking roof.
Can you build the website too?
Yes — that's our most common flow. We hand the Figma library off, then our vibe-coded sites team ships a Next.js build on top of it. Same studio, same accountable lead, no Figma-to-dev translation loss. That's a separate engagement priced separately.
How do you brief photography?
Shot list, mood board, lighting style, post-process LUT (a Lightroom preset we share with the photographer). We prefer one editorial-style shoot over twenty stock images. For founder-led brands, an iPhone in your home office with our shot list often beats hiring a photographer who doesn't know your story.
What if our existing brand book has 80 pages we like?
Honestly, that's usually a sign the book was made to impress, not to operate. We can audit what's worth keeping (typography rules, semantic colour, motion specs) and build a 20-page operating version on top. The 80-page version goes in the archive.
