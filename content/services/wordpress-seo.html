Questions

Common questions

Will pruning hurt our existing rankings?
If done badly, yes. We never delete a URL without a mapped 301 redirect to the closest topic match. Pages with backlinks get merged, not killed. Most prune projects see a small dip in week one and a recovery to baseline by week six, then growth as the remaining pages get more authority per page.
Can you migrate us off WordPress?
Yes if you want, but rarely worth it. WordPress is still the fastest path to a content engine because of the block editor + plugin ecosystem. We migrate to Next.js or Astro only when speed-critical or when a headless setup is required. For 80% of clients, fixing WordPress beats migrating.
What does "AEO" actually change vs traditional SEO?
Three structural shifts. One: direct-answer paragraphs in the first 80 words. Two: explicit citations with linked sources. Three: schema that LLMs can parse without ambiguity (Article + FAQ + HowTo + author + publisher). Traditional SEO optimises for click-through; AEO optimises for being quoted without a click.
How do you write briefs without sounding generic?
We voice-lock with three samples from your existing best posts, then write briefs that include exact phrases, named clients, and pain points your buyers will recognise. We run a lint pass on every draft to kill AI-tells like "leverage" and "robust".
Do you handle backlinks?
We don't buy backlinks or do guest-post farms. We do build out HARO replies, podcast pitches, and digital-PR pieces that earn citations naturally. Backlinks are a downstream effect of doing good work and getting cited; they're not a product you should buy.
