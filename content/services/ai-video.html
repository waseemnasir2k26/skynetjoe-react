Questions

Common questions

Can you make me a faceless AI avatar?
We can, but we usually argue against it for founder-led brands. Avatar reels under-perform face-to-camera by a factor of two to four on engagement, and viewers can tell. For e-commerce or news-style channels where there is no founder, faceless is fine and we'll set up HeyGen or Synthesia.
Do I need a fancy camera?
No. The reference reel that opened this pipeline was shot on a DJI Pocket 3 in handheld mode at noon, full sun, with no microphone. Captions and EQ do most of the heavy lifting. An iPhone 14 or later is enough.
Can I run it without a GPU?
Yes, on a CPU it's slower but works. Whisper has a CPU-friendly model. Pollinations runs in the cloud. ffmpeg uses CPU by default. A modern laptop runs the full nine-stage pipeline on a 60-second clip in roughly five minutes.
What about copyright on music?
We default to the Mixkit free tier and clearly-licensed Epidemic Sound tracks if you have a subscription. We never use TikTok-trending audio in the auto-pipeline because it ages fast and creates copyright strikes on cross-posted YouTube uploads.
Can my team edit clips after the pipeline runs?
Yes. The pipeline writes a Premiere-compatible XML and a CapCut JSON. Editors can re-time captions, swap B-roll, and re-export. That said, most clients post the pipeline output as-is once we've locked the look.
