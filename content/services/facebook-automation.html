Questions

Common questions

Isn't Facebook dead for organic reach?
Dead for broadcasts, alive for groups and Reels. Page reach for static-image posts collapsed in 2020. But Facebook Groups, Facebook Reels, and DM-driven funnels still convert. We avoid the page-broadcast trap and use the surfaces that still work.
Should we run paid ads alongside this?
Eventually yes, but not while the organic side is broken. Spending on ads while the comment-to-DM funnel is dead burns money. We get organic working first, then layer ads on the same funnel because the cost-per-lead drops.
What about Facebook Reels — separate or part of this?
Part of this. The same reels going to TikTok and Instagram cross-post to Facebook Reels. Facebook Reels reach for a sub-10k page can be five to ten times the page-post reach right now. We bake the cross-post into the schedule.
Can you take over our existing group?
We don't moderate as ourselves. We set up the revival sequence, document the rules, train your moderator, and stay on retainer for monthly tune-ups. Group culture has to come from inside the community, not an outside agency.
How does this play with our Signal strategy?
Facebook DM is the cold layer. ManyChat handles the first reply and qualifying. When the buyer is warm, we lateral to Signal via a Cloud API template. Signal is the booked-call layer. Both share the same CRM tag set.
