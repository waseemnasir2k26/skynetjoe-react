Questions

Common questions

Will my account get banned?
Not if we build it with official APIs. The bans you see online come from session-hijack tools that scrape DMs or auto-follow at scale. We use Meta Graph, TikTok Business, and LinkedIn's official APIs. They are rate-limited and slower, but they are also approved and audited.
Do you write the content too?
If you want. Our default is wiring the engine and training your team to feed it. We have a sister content workflow that ships 5 to 10 posts a week in your voice using a locked template — that's a separate engagement. Most clients run their own content and just want the plumbing.
What about LinkedIn — can we automate it?
Selectively. LinkedIn is the most aggressive about banning. We use the official API for posting, the official content library for scheduling, and we do not touch automated outreach or connection requests. Connections still go through a human.
How does the Signal side stay compliant?
We use the Signal Cloud API, not WA Web scrapers. That means business-initiated messages need approved templates (we get them approved), and any session message has to be inside the 24-hour window. We document every flow so you can pass a Meta business audit.
Can you measure conversions, not just vanity metrics?
Yes. We tag every keyword-triggered DM with a source label, pass it through to your CRM, and join it back to closed deal value in Looker Studio. The weekly digest shows DMs to booked calls to closed work, not "impressions up 14 percent".
