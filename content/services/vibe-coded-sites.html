Questions

Common questions

Can my non-technical team edit the site after you ship?
Yes if we wire a CMS. We default to Sanity for editor-friendly cases and content folders in the repo for technical founders who'd rather just open a markdown file. Most clients pick Sanity for blog/case studies and leave marketing pages in code.
What if we want to keep our WordPress and add a Next.js sub-site?
Common pattern. We deploy Next.js on a subdomain or use Vercel's edge router to put Next on /shop while WordPress stays on /. We've done this for clients who want the marketing site rebuilt without touching the existing blog or membership area.
Will you migrate our content from Wix or Squarespace?
Yes. We export your existing content, restructure into the new info architecture, set up 301 redirects, and run a crawl pre- and post-launch to verify zero broken links. SEO is rarely lost in these migrations when done with redirects mapped.
