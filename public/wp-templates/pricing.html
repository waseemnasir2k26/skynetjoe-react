FAQ

Pricing questions we get often

Why isn't there an enterprise tier?
Because every "enterprise tier" we've ever been quoted was a generic Flagship with a different number on it. If your scope genuinely needs more than the Flagship can hold, we'll quote the delta on top — line by line, in writing, before the kickoff. No mystery tier.
Can I split the Flagship into two payments?
Yes. The default is 50% at kickoff and 50% at ship. For larger Flagship scopes (multi-site or multi-brand), we can offer three payments tied to milestones. We don't run subscriptions on a one-time fee.
What happens if you go over the ship window?
If the delay is on us, the retainer month is free or we credit the equivalent hours on a one-time build. If the delay is on your side (asset hand-off, account access, brand approval), we'll pause the clock with a written log — no surprise extension fees. The ship window in your contract is the one we hit.
Do you offer revenue-share or equity in place of cash?
No. We've taken three rev-share deals and zero of them ended cleanly. Our pricing is structured to be cash-affordable at the size of business that benefits — under that line, the work doesn't pay back fast enough either way.
Can I see the contract before I send a brief?
Yes. Drop a one-line request via /contact and we'll send the standard MSA + SOW template before you commit. It's two pages. Everyone reads it.
What payment methods do you accept?
Wise, Payoneer, PayPal, Stripe invoice, direct USD wire, or bank transfer in IDR / GBP / EUR / SAR / AED. Crypto on request. We invoice from a registered entity — no PayPal "friends and family" hacks.
