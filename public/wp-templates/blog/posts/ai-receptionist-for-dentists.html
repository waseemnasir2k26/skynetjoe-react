What does a missed call mean to a practice?

New patients often decide fast. The first practice that picks up, or replies helpfully, tends to get the booking. Front desks are busy with patients in the building, lunch breaks and closing time, so calls roll to voicemail more than anyone likes.

Hiring more reception hours is expensive, and it still does not cover midnight. An AI receptionist fills the gaps rather than replacing your team.

Waking up to a list of booked appointments and a short list of flagged urgent cases is a different start to the day than a voicemail box full of people you now have to chase.

How the AI receptionist works

Answer the call, understand the request, book into the real calendar, confirm by text, and escalate emergencies to a human.
StepWhat happensTypical tool
1. AnswerThe call is picked up by an AI voice assistant when your team cannot answer.Voice provider connected to n8n
2. UnderstandIt asks what the caller needs: check-up, pain, whitening, existing patient.AI model
3. Check the calendarIt looks for real free slots in your practice calendar.Google Calendar or practice software
4. BookThe appointment is created with the caller's name and number.Calendar API
5. ConfirmA text confirmation with date, time and address is sent to the caller.SMS provider
6. Flag emergenciesPain, swelling or trauma are marked urgent and sent to your team for the morning, or to an on-call number if you have one.Notification

The assistant stays in its lane. It books and informs; it does not give clinical advice. For anything medical it says the team will call back.

Callers are told they are speaking with an automated assistant, which is both honest and increasingly a legal requirement in some places.

What you need to get started

  • A calendar that can be read and written by automation.
  • A list of appointment types and their lengths.
  • Your rules for emergencies and who gets alerted.
  • Clear wording of the call greeting, including the automated assistant disclosure.

Honest limits

  • Voice AI can mishear accents and noisy lines, so it confirms key details back to the caller.
  • It cannot give clinical advice and should not try.
  • Patient data rules apply. Choose providers that meet your local requirements.
  • Some practice software has no API, which limits direct booking.

Frequently asked questions

Can the AI receptionist book into my real calendar?

Yes, if your calendar or practice software exposes an API. It checks free slots and creates the appointment directly.

What happens with a dental emergency?

The call is flagged as urgent and passed to your team, or to an on-call number if you set one. The assistant does not give clinical advice.

Does it replace my front desk?

No. It covers missed calls and after-hours so your team can focus on patients in the building.

Will patients know they are speaking to AI?

They should be told. We include a clear disclosure in the greeting.
