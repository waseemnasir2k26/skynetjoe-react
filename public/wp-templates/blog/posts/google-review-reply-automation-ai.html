Why does replying to reviews matter?

A reply shows future customers that someone is paying attention. A calm answer to a bad review often says more about your business than the review itself. Unanswered complaints, on the other hand, look like you do not care.

Writing thoughtful replies by hand takes time and is easy to put off, and a rushed reply to an angry customer can make things worse.

Reviews also contain information. Patterns such as slow service on Fridays or a dish people love are useful, and a log of every review makes those patterns visible instead of buried in a feed.

How the review responder works

New review in, sentiment checked, tailored reply drafted and posted. Unhappy customers can be routed to you first.
StepWhat happensTypical tool
1. DetectA new review on your Google Business Profile triggers the workflow.Google Business Profile API
2. ReadAI works out whether the review is positive, mixed or negative, and what it mentions.n8n plus an AI model
3. DraftA reply is written in your tone, using the reviewer's name and the specific points they raised.AI model with your style guide
4. Rules checkThe draft is checked against your rules, such as no promises, no refunds, no personal data.n8n checks
5. Post or holdPositive reviews are posted automatically. Negative ones can be held for your approval.Google Business Profile API
6. LogEvery review and reply is logged so you can see themes over time.Sheet

We usually recommend auto-posting positive replies and holding negative ones for a quick human check. It keeps the speed where it is safe and the care where it matters.

The style guide is the heart of it: a few examples of replies you like, words you never use, and how you handle complaints.

What you need to get started

  • A verified Google Business Profile with API access.
  • A short style guide with example replies.
  • A rule for what the AI must never promise, such as refunds or discounts.
  • Someone to approve held replies.

Honest limits

  • Replies can sound generic if the style guide is thin. We tune it on your past reviews first.
  • It cannot resolve the underlying complaint. It only responds well.
  • Google's API access can require approval, which can take time.
  • Fake or abusive reviews need a separate report process.

Frequently asked questions

Will the reply sound like me?

It is guided by your style guide and sample replies, then tested against past reviews. You can review early drafts before any go live.

What about angry reviews?

The workflow can hold negative replies for your approval so you control anything sensitive.

Can the AI promise refunds?

No. We set rules so it never offers refunds, discounts or other commitments. Those stay with you.

The video says under a minute for automatic ones. Held replies post as soon as you approve them.
