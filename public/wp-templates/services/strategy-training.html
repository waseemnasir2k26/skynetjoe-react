Questions

Common questions

Can we train just the leadership team first?
Yes — this is often the right move. We run a "founder + 3 leaders" mini-engagement so the top of the company learns to ship AI workflows themselves before they ask the team to. The leadership cohort tends to adopt fastest because they have direct skin in the savings.
What if our team is already using AI tools "informally"?
Common starting point. Step one is usually surfacing the shadow AI usage — who's already pasting customer data into the free ChatGPT, what tools have unaudited approval. Then we formalise: paid tiers with data-retention controls, a written policy, sanctioned workflows. Most teams sleep better after this step.
Do you do industry-specific training (legal, medical, finance)?
Yes, with a different rigor bar. Medical = HIPAA-mode training, no PHI in any free tier, only AWS Bedrock or Azure OpenAI with BAAs. Legal = work-product privilege concerns. Finance = SOX considerations. The playbooks change but the workshop format holds.
How do you handle remote vs in-person?
Both work. In-person sessions adopt about 20% faster because of the social pressure of pair-work in a room. Remote requires tighter facilitation — smaller groups (4 max), more breakout time, mandatory cameras-on. We've delivered both formats successfully.
What if someone refuses to engage with AI?
It happens. We don't force adoption. We make the case once with the data (hours saved, work quality), then let leadership decide whether that role needs to evolve. The 30-day metrics make the conversation factual rather than ideological.
