What is e-commerce automation?

E-commerce automation is an integration layer connecting Shopify, Stripe, Klaviyo, a 3PL or fulfilment system, and customer support into one workflow so a single order trigger handles fulfilment, refunds, abandoned cart, returns, and support triage. It serves stores doing 30+ orders a day whose ops still run on tabs and spreadsheets. SkynetLabs builds the n8n Shopify-Stripe-Klaviyo stack with operations dashboard. Engagements from $1,500.

Order-to-fulfilment pipeline

Shopify order webhook → n8n routes by SKU → inventory check → ShipStation label → tracking number into Klaviyo confirmation flow. End-to-end under 90 seconds, no human touches in the middle.

Returns and refunds workflow

One-click return portal, return label automation, refund processing in Stripe, inventory restocking in Shopify, customer notification at every step. Replaces three tabs and a sticky note.

Operations dashboard

Today's revenue, today's orders, top SKU, stuck shipments, refund rate, top-3 product reviews. Looker Studio fed by n8n daily exports. Email digest at 7am.

Low-stock and reorder triggers

SKU drops below threshold → Slack alert to the founder, draft reorder PO emailed to supplier, COGS sheet updated. No more "we sold out yesterday" surprises.

Support escalation routing

Gorgias or Zendesk inbound → AI classifier (shipping question vs refund vs complaint) → auto-reply with tracking, escalation to human for nuance, satisfaction survey post-close.

Questions

Common questions

Can you work with WooCommerce or BigCommerce, not just Shopify?
Yes. The pattern is the same: webhook out of the platform, n8n handles routing, Stripe handles money, Klaviyo or a fork handles email. WooCommerce works well for content-heavy stores; BigCommerce for B2B with complex pricing tiers. We default to Shopify because most clients are already on it.
What about subscriptions and recurring billing?
We wire Recharge or Bold for Shopify, or Stripe Billing directly for headless stores. Both feed into the same n8n hub for tag management, churn-prediction flows, and dunning emails when cards fail.
Will you slow my store down with all this automation?
Opposite. Most of what we replace is Shopify apps loading scripts on the storefront. By moving abandoned-cart and inventory logic to background n8n workflows, the storefront actually gets faster. Average LCP improvement: 30—50% on stores we've migrated.
Can we handle Black Friday on this stack?
Yes — and that's when it pays off the most. We rehearse BFCM in November with a load-test against 20x normal volume, queue-buffer the inventory check, and set fallback paths so a Shopify webhook delay doesn't break fulfilment. Built for spike days.
What's the smallest store worth doing this for?
If you ship under 10 orders a day, you're better off with a clean Shopify + Klaviyo setup and a part-time VA. We earn our keep above 30 orders a day or when the founder is doing fulfilment themselves and wants to stop. Past that volume, the time saved easily covers the build cost.
