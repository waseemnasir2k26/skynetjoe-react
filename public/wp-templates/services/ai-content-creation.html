Questions

Common questions

Will my content stop sounding like ChatGPT?
Yes — that's the explicit goal. The voice linter hard-fails on 60+ AI tells (leverage, robust, seamless, harness, delve, "in today's", "in the realm of", em-dash overuse, rule-of-three, vague attributions). A draft can't ship if it triggers any of them. Add to the banned list as new tells emerge.
Can my VA or junior writer use this system?
That's the whole point. The voice profile is explicit enough that a competent junior can draft in your voice on day one. The linter catches drift before you ever see the draft. Most of our clients run the engine with a $500/mo VA after the system is locked.
What if my voice changes over the year?
Voice drifts naturally as you grow. The retainer includes a quarterly refresh: we re-sample your 20 best recent posts, update the profile, retire stale hooks, add new banned phrases. Your voice ages with you instead of getting frozen in time.
Do you write the posts, or just build the system?
We build the system. Drafting we hand off — either to your VA, your in-house writer, or a content retainer (separate engagement). Our job is to make sure whoever drafts ships in your voice, not theirs.
What if I don't have 30 past posts to sample from?
Common with founders just starting to post. We do a 90-minute voice interview, transcribe it, and sample your spoken voice instead. Most founders' written voice ends up 80% identical to their spoken voice once we strip the conference-talk filler.
