What are Zapier and Make automations?

Zapier and Make are managed integration-platform-as-a-service (iPaaS) tools that connect SaaS apps via no-code triggers, filters, and actions — Zapier for simple linear flows, Make for visual multi-branch scenarios with iteration. They serve teams without engineers who need to wire 5-50 apps together. SkynetLabs audits, prunes, and rebuilds Zapier/Make stacks with AI steps, error alerting, and runbook documentation. Questions

Common questions

Why not just move everything to n8n and skip Zapier?
Sometimes that is the right answer — and we'll say so. But n8n needs hosting, a deploy story, and a person who can read a JSON error. Zapier is right when the team is small, the connectors are exotic, and you'd rather pay a SaaS fee than a DevOps fee. Make sits in the middle. We pick by team and by load, not by ideology.
Will the audit alone reduce our task burn?
Usually yes. About 30 to 50 percent of Zaps in a long-running account are firing on triggers nobody uses anymore. Killing those drops the task count without touching the work that matters. We've seen a Pro account drop to Starter inside 30 days.
What's the difference between Make scenarios and Zapier Zaps in practice?
Zapier is per-task billing and one-trigger-one-action thinking. Make charges per operation and assumes you'll branch, iterate, and bulk-process. Bulk jobs (300 rows nightly) cost a fortune in Zapier and are pennies in Make. Real-time single-event jobs are cleaner in Zapier.
Can you support webhooks from custom apps?
Yes. We've connected custom Laravel apps, Rails monoliths, and Bubble back-ends to both platforms via webhook gateways. The pattern is the same: named endpoint, versioned payload, replay capability, request logging in a Google Sheet for audit.
How long until you're out and we're self-sufficient?
Most teams take 30 to 45 days to feel confident editing scenarios themselves. We hand off Loom walkthroughs per scenario and a one-page runbook. After that, the retainer is optional — we keep maybe a third of clients on it after handoff because they prefer outsourcing the monthly review.
