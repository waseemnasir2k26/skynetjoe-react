What is YouTube automation?

YouTube automation is a production system that runs a faceless or founder-led channel using AI for topic research, scripts, voiceover, visuals, and thumbnail testing. It serves operators who want owned-media reach without filming themselves daily. SkynetLabs builds the full stack — VidIQ-style gap analysis, Claude/GPT-4o scripts written to your voice profile, ElevenLabs voiceover, layered B-roll, and three-thumbnail A/B/C testing via the YouTube experiment API. Questions

Common questions

Will YouTube demonetise a faceless AI channel?
Only if the channel is low-effort reuploads or near-identical to other channels. YouTube updated its rules in 2024 specifically to target template-farm content. Our pipeline produces high-effort, value-add videos: original scripts, voice, B-roll layered with motion graphics, custom thumbnails. Channels we've shipped have passed monetisation review.
Can I keep my own voice in the videos without filming?
Yes. We can clone your voice with ElevenLabs (you give consent, you control the model). The output is close enough that listeners on YouTube reviews can't reliably tell. We avoid voice cloning for political or medical channels for ethical reasons.
How long until the channel starts earning AdSense?
Honest answer: depends on niche and consistency. We've seen channels hit the 1,000 sub / 4,000 watch-hour threshold in 90 days when the niche has demand and the consistency holds. We do not promise revenue, and we never quote dollar figures upfront.
Do you handle the sponsorships?
We don't pitch sponsors for you. We do bake in sponsor-read templates and the audio levels so that when you land a sponsor, dropping the read into the script is one block. Sponsorship outreach lives with you or your manager.
What if I want to do shorts only, no long-form?
Shorts run through our reel pipeline. We typically recommend a mixed channel: shorts feed the algorithm and surface the channel, long-form converts subscribers and earns ad revenue. Pure-short channels can grow fast but cap at a lower revenue ceiling.
