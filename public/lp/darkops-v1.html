FAQ / pre-sales

Things ops directors actually ask.

FAQ_01 / INTEGRATIONSWhich TMS and telematics platforms do you integrate with?

Out of the box: Samsara, Motive, Geotab, FreightOS, McLeod, AscendTMS, Truckstop, Project44. Any platform with a REST API or webhook surface — we wire via n8n. ELD/DOT logging compliant. If you're on a paper TMS, we migrate you to a lightweight portal first as part of the build.

FAQ_02 / DEPLOYMENTCan the console run on-prem or in our private cloud?

Yes. We deploy to AWS, Azure, GCP, or on-prem Kubernetes. Voice AI can run via OpenAI, Azure OpenAI Service (HIPAA-aligned), or self-hosted Llama 3.1 70B for air-gapped deployments. Defense and manufacturing clients use the self-hosted path.

FAQ_03 / MULTI-TENANTHow does multi-tenant work for 3PLs and freight brokers?

Row-level tenant isolation, per-tenant API keys, custom branding per yard. One console deployment, N customers — used by 3PLs managing 6-200 shipper accounts on a single instance. Per-tenant data exports and billing pass-through available.

FAQ_04 / ESCALATIONWhat happens when the AI escalates? Who answers?

Confidence threshold + escalation tree. Below 0.82 confidence, AI warm-transfers to your on-call dispatcher with full context (caller ID, intent, transcript, suggested action). Tier 1: dispatcher. Tier 2: ops manager. Tier 3: COO via SMS + email. Median escalation to human: 27 seconds.

FAQ_05 / SLAWhat is your SLA and how is it enforced?

Tier 1: 99.5%. Tier 2: 99.9%. Tier 3: 99.95%. Monitored 24/7. P1 incident response: 15 min (Tier 3). P2: 1 hour. Voice AI median latency: 480ms. Public status page at status.skynetlabs.ops. Penalty credits on missed SLA — written into the MSA, not the marketing page.

FAQ_06 / PRICINGHow is pricing structured — per seat or per yard?

Per yard / per plant / per fleet boundary — not per seat. Unlimited dispatchers and ops staff on every tier. Voice AI minutes are pooled across yards. No per-call inbound fees. Outbound minutes at cost pass-through. Full pricing is on this page — three public tiers, no quote games.
