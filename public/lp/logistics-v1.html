Frequently asked.

Do you only work with US logistics operators?

We specialize in US carriers, freight brokers, and 5-50 truck fleets — that's where our AI dispatcher and TMS integrations are tuned. We also serve Canadian operators. Outside North America, we'll refer.

Can the AI dispatcher integrate with my TMS?

Yes. Out of the box: Samsara, Motive, McLeod, AscendTMS, Truckstop. Anything with a REST API or webhook — we wire it via n8n. If your TMS is paper-based, we move you to GoHighLevel + a lightweight portal first.

How fast can the AI dispatcher go live?

Live in 14 days from signed brief. Days 1-3: voice training on your lanes + rates. Days 4-9: portal + CRM + TMS wiring. Days 10-14: pilot calls, tuning, soft launch. Day 14: live.

What if my drivers are not tech-savvy?

The driver app is text-message based by default — no install. Rate con arrives by SMS. Check-calls arrive by SMS. POD upload by SMS reply with photo. The complexity sits server-side, not in the cab.

What does it replace?

Typically the 4-tool stack: separate CRM + load board + email blaster + WhatsApp/SMS manager. We collapse to one connected pipeline you own. Saves $400-1200/mo in SaaS + 14+ hrs/wk in admin.

Do I own the system after launch?

Yes. Repo, GHL sub-account, n8n workflows, voice training data — all transferred to you. Cancel retainer anytime; the system keeps running. We hate vendor lock-in because we've been on the other side of it.
