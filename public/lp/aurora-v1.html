The three quiet leaks

Most clinics don't lose to competitors. They lose to their own front desk.

No. 01

Front desk burnout

Your receptionist juggles seven things at once — checking in, calling back, confirming, billing, fielding the dentist. By 3pm she stops smiling. By Friday she's drafting a resignation. Patients feel it.

Avg cost · $4,800/mo in turnover + lost goodwill
No. 02

Missed-call leakage

Every unanswered call is a $400-1,800 case walking to the practice down the street. After-hours, lunch hour, weekends — voicemail loses to the clinic that picks up. Even when you do answer, half the leads drop into a Post-it abyss.

Avg loss · $11K-28K/mo in unbooked revenue
No. 03

Same-day cancel chaos

The 10am cancels at 9:47. Your hygienist is paid either way. By the time the front desk dials through the call-list, the chair sits empty for 90 minutes. Multiply that across the week — that's a full salary gone.

Avg loss · $6K-14K/mo in idle chair-hours

The honest answers

Frequently asked, candidly answered.

Is the AI receptionist HIPAA-considerate?

Yes. We never store PHI in the voice transcript layer. Sensitive data routes directly to your encrypted PMS — Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Jane App, Mindbody. The AI books, reschedules and confirms. Your PMS holds the chart. We sign a BAA on the Practice tier and above.

Will it integrate with my practice management software?

Yes. We have native connectors for Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, Jane App, Mindbody, ClinicSense and Practice Better. If your PMS has a REST API or HL7 surface, we wire it. If it's paper-based, we'll move you to a lightweight portal first — we won't force a replatform you don't need.

What will the AI sound like to my patients?

Natural, warm and never robotic — we hand-tune the voice on your clinic's tone. Chatty New England family dental, calm West Coast med-spa, clinical East Coast specialty. ElevenLabs voice paired with custom GPT logic. You'll listen to fifteen sample calls before we go live, and we adjust until you can't tell.

How does same-day cancel rescue actually work?

The moment a cancellation hits your calendar, our system pings your short-list (reactivation pool + waitlist + flagged "would love a sooner spot" patients) by SMS with the open slot. They book with a single tap. Most chairs are filled within 90 minutes. The hygienist never sits idle.

What if my front desk team feels threatened?

Honest answer — they shouldn't be, and we'll bring them in from day one. Aurora handles overflow, after-hours, and the soul-crushing repetitive booking work. Your humans get to do the work humans are best at — greeting, in-clinic upsell, the relational part. Clinics that include staff in onboarding see 2× faster adoption.

What happens after launch — do we own everything?

Yes. Repo, automation workflows, voice training data, CRM sub-account — all transferred to you on day 14. Cancel the retainer anytime; the system keeps running. We hate vendor lock-in because we've been on the other side of it. Built once, owned forever.
